Form History

Every Google Form you have answered, and every job application you have sent, in one list.

Install Privacy policy Source code

What it does

  • Remembers forms. Each Google Form you open is listed, and marked Submitted once you send it.
  • Tracks job applications sent on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby, with the job title and date.
  • Tells you what is still open. Once a day it checks whether each form still takes responses and each job posting is still up.
  • Works across your computers. Sign in with Google in Chrome and Edge on each computer, and they share one list.

Private by design

There is no Form History server. Your list stays in your browser and, if you choose to sign in, in a hidden file in your own Google Drive that only this extension can open. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Form History is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google, Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby; their names are used only to say which sites it works with.
