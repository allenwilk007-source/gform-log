Privacy policy

Form History browser extension. Effective 23 October 2026.

Form History lists the Google Forms you have opened or submitted and the job applications you have sent, so you can find them again. This page says exactly what it reads, what it keeps, where that goes, and how to delete it. The source code is public at github.com/allenwilk007-source/gform-log.

What it reads

  • Browser history. The addresses and titles of Google Forms pages and of pages on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby job sites. Every other page in your history is skipped without being stored.
  • Pages as you browse them, but only on Google Forms and those three job sites: the address and title, to record a form you open or an application you send.
  • Page text on those job sites, only just after you press Submit or Apply, to see whether a thank-you message appeared. The text is checked in your browser and never stored or sent anywhere.
  • A Google Takeout file, only if you choose to import one. It is read in your browser and only the Google Forms and job entries are kept.

What it keeps

For each form or application: its title, the link to it, when you last opened it, when you submitted it, whether it was seen open or closed, and the names of your browsers that saw it. It never keeps your answers, the contents of forms, résumés, or anything you typed.

Where it is kept

  • In your browser, in the extension's own storage.
  • In your browser's own sync, which copies extension data between browsers signed in to the same Google account (Chrome) or Microsoft account (Edge). This is run by Google or Microsoft under their own terms.
  • In your Google Drive, only if you click "Sign in with Google". The list is saved as one file in Drive's hidden app-data folder, which only Form History can see. It cannot see, open or change any of your other files.

There is no Form History server. The developer never receives your list, your history or your Google account details.

What it sends, and to whom

ToWhat, and why
Google Forms and the three job sitesA normal page request for each item in your list, once a day or when you press "Check now", to see whether the form still takes responses or the posting is still up. It is sent as you, with your existing sign-in, just as if you opened the page.
Google sign-in and Google DriveOnly if you sign in: to get permission for the app-data folder and to read and write the one shared file.

Nothing else. No analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting.

Google user data

When you sign in, Form History asks Google for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata, which lets it keep its own file in a hidden folder of your Drive. It uses that permission only to store and sync your Form History list between your browsers. It does not transfer that data to anyone, use it for advertising, sell it, or let any person read it.

Form History's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The sign-in token is kept in memory only, never on disk, and lasts one hour. "Sign out" revokes it.

Deleting your data

  • Forget everything (under More options) deletes the list from this browser, from your other synced browsers, and from the shared Drive file.
  • Sign out stops sharing and revokes Google access.
  • Removing the extension deletes its data from that browser.
  • The Drive file can also be deleted in Google Drive: Settings, then Manage apps, then Form History, then Options, then Delete hidden app data. You can withdraw access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sale of data

Your data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and is not used to decide creditworthiness or for lending.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. A change that collects more data will ask for your permission first.
